Editorial policy

How health content on THEGLODOC is written, checked and updated.

Last updated 2026-11-26

Who writes the content

Health content on this site and on THEGLODOC channels is written or reviewed by Dr. Athmika Prakash (BHMS (Bachelor of Homoeopathic Medicine and Surgery), FMC (Fellowship in Medical Cosmetology), Certified Consultant Medical Cosmetologist). Each article names its author and reviewer and shows when it was published and last reviewed.

Sources

Articles are based on recognised sources such as the World Health Organization, the Indian Council of Medical Research, the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, national and international clinical guidelines, and peer-reviewed research. Sources are listed at the end of each article.

Accuracy and limits

  • Content explains health topics in general terms. It is not a diagnosis or a treatment plan for any individual.
  • Where evidence is limited or uncertain, the article says so.
  • Content does not promise results, and avoids sensational headlines.

Review and updates

Articles are reviewed at least once a year, and sooner when guidance changes. If you spot an error, email drathmika999@gmail.com and it will be checked and corrected.

Paid partnerships

Editorial content is never paid for. Paid partnerships are labelled clearly, follow the standards on the brand partnerships page, and do not influence health articles. Dr. Athmika does not endorse prescription medicines, drugs or medical devices.

Use of artificial intelligence

Tools may help with drafting or formatting, but every health statement is checked by Dr. Athmika against the sources before it is published.
