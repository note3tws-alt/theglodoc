Consultations with Dr. Athmika

Online and offline consultations for everyday health, women's health, skin and hair.

  • 4,000+patient consultations completed
  • Onlinefrom wherever you are
  • Offlinein person, by appointment

Online consultation

Speak with Dr. Athmika remotely about a health, skin or hair concern. It suits questions that can be discussed and guided without a physical examination.

Offline consultation

Meet Dr. Athmika in person when a concern needs to be examined. Details of where and when are shared when you enquire.

What you can discuss

  • Everyday health questions and routine checks
  • Women's health concerns
  • Skin and hair concerns
  • Medical cosmetology procedures: what they involve and whether they suit you

Limits of online consultation

  • A video or phone call cannot replace a physical examination or tests. You may be asked to visit in person, or to see another specialist.
  • It is not for emergencies. If you have severe symptoms, go to the nearest hospital.
  • Please do not rely on videos or website content as personal medical advice.

How to enquire

  1. Send an enquiry on the contact page, or message @the.glodoc on Instagram.
  2. Tell us whether you prefer online or offline, and what you would like to discuss in a line or two.
  3. The team replies with availability and the next step. Fees are shared on enquiry.

This page is for general education. It is not a diagnosis or personal medical advice, and it does not replace a consultation with a doctor. If you are unwell or worried about a symptom, please see a doctor in person. In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital.
