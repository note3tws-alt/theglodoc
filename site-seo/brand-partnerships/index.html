Brand partnerships with Dr. Athmika

Doctor-led health education for hospitals, health-tech, wellness brands and agencies in India, the UAE and the Gulf.

As THEGLODOC, she explains women's health, everyday health, skin and hair in short, plain-language videos for people who want health information they can trust. For healthcare organisations and responsible health brands, she offers doctor-led, education-first content that is checked for accuracy and clearly disclosed.

Ways to collaborate

  • Health awareness campaigns. Short videos and Story sets that explain a health topic, a screening or a habit, for awareness days, hospital outreach and public health messaging.
  • Educational content series. A multi-part series that explains a topic properly, where one Reel is not enough.
  • Expert interviews and podcasts. Interviews, panels, Lives and podcast episodes on women's health, skin, hair and everyday health.
  • Events and speaking. Talks, workshops and health camps for organisations, colleges and corporate wellness programmes.
  • Product education. For suitable wellness, skincare and haircare products: what the product is, who it is and is not for, and how to use it sensibly. Clearly labelled as a paid partnership.

Why a doctor-led voice

People increasingly look to doctors on social media to tell them what is true. That trust is the reason brands want doctor-creators, and the reason it has to be protected. Dr. Athmika's content is built on her medical training, her experience of 4,000+ patient consultations, and a simple rule: explain clearly, never overstate.

What she does not do

  • Promotion or endorsement of prescription medicines, drugs or medical devices.
  • Claims of cure, guaranteed results, or words such as "best", "100%" or "no side effects".
  • Patient testimonials, before-and-after images or patient stories without documented consent.
  • Fear-based messaging, or content that encourages self-diagnosis or self-medication.
  • Supplements or products whose claims cannot be supported by evidence.
  • Undisclosed paid content. Every paid partnership is labelled.

How a partnership works

  1. Enquiry. You share the brief using the form below.
  2. Fit check. Dr. Athmika checks whether the product or campaign fits her audience and her professional standards. Not every enquiry is accepted.
  3. Claims review. Every claim is checked against evidence and the brand's approved information. Wording is agreed in writing.
  4. Approvals. Where a campaign needs regulatory approval, for example an advertiser permit or a health authority approval in the UAE, it proceeds only once that is in place.
  5. Publish and report. Content goes live with clear disclosure, and performance figures are shared after the campaign.

Questions brands ask

Does Dr. Athmika accept every partnership?

No. Each enquiry is checked for fit with her audience, the evidence behind the product, and the professional and advertising rules that apply to doctors. She declines work that does not meet those standards.

Which countries do you work with?

Enquiries are welcome from organisations in India, the UAE and the wider Gulf. Dr. Athmika is based in Kannur, Kerala. For campaigns aimed at the UAE, the required permits and approvals are confirmed before any content is produced.

Is paid content disclosed?

Yes. Every paid partnership is labelled clearly at the start of the content, in line with ASCI guidelines in India and the rules of the platform.

Can she promote medicines or treatments?

No. She does not endorse prescription medicines, drugs or medical devices, and she does not promise treatment outcomes.

What are the rates?

Rates depend on the format, the number of deliverables and usage rights. They are shared after the brief is reviewed.

How soon will we hear back?

The team aims to reply to complete enquiries within a few working days.
