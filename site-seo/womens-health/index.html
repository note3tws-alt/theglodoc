Women's health, explained clearly.

Periods, hormones, checkups and pregnancy myths, in plain language.

Women's health questions are often surrounded by myths and embarrassment. Dr. Athmika answers them in plain language, so you know what is common, what deserves attention, and when to see a doctor.

Questions this section covers

  • What are the common causes of irregular periods?
  • What is PCOS, and how is it different from PCOD?
  • What are the signs of a hormonal imbalance?
  • Which health checks are worth doing before marriage?
  • What are the common myths about pregnancy?
  • What should a routine women's health checkup include?

Dr. Athmika's articles on these questions are written and reviewed by her before they are published here. In the meantime you can follow her on Instagram or enquire about a consultation.
