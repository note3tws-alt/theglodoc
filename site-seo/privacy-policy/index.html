Privacy policy

What information this site collects and how it is used.

Last updated 2026-11-26

What we collect

  • Enquiry forms. When you send an enquiry, we receive what you enter, such as your name, email, organisation and message. Forms are processed by our hosting provider, Netlify.
  • Analytics. If analytics are enabled, Google Analytics collects anonymous usage data such as pages visited, device type and approximate location, using cookies.
  • Email. If you email us, we receive your email address and message.

How we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, to manage consultations and partnerships, and to understand how the site is used. We do not sell your information.

Health information

Please do not send medical history, reports or other sensitive health information through the forms. If a consultation needs it, you will be told how to share it safely.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to respond and for related records, and then deleted.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information you sent us, email drathmika999@gmail.com. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
