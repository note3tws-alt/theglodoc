About Dr. Athmika Prakash

Doctor, certified consultant medical cosmetologist, health educator and content creator.

Who she is

Dr. Athmika Prakash is a doctor, certified consultant medical cosmetologist and health educator based in Kannur, Kerala. She shares clear, everyday health education through short videos on Instagram and YouTube under the name THEGLODOC.

Her work focuses on questions people ask every day: women's health, gut and general health, skin and hair, and what medical cosmetology procedures involve and who they suit.

Qualifications

  • BHMS, Bachelor of Homoeopathic Medicine and Surgery
  • FMC, Fellowship in Medical Cosmetology
  • Certified Consultant Medical Cosmetologist

Experience

  • 4,000+ patient consultations completed, online and offline
  • Health educator and content creator on Instagram (12.3K followers) and YouTube

What she covers

  • Women's health: periods, hormones, checkups and pregnancy myths
  • Gut and everyday health: digestion, routine tests and common deficiencies
  • Skin and hair: common concerns, myths and what to ask a doctor
  • Medical cosmetology: what procedures are and who they suit

Work with her

Patients can enquire about a consultation. Hospitals, health brands and agencies can read about brand partnerships or see the media kit. Everyone can watch her videos or follow along on Instagram.

This page is for general education. It is not a diagnosis or personal medical advice, and it does not replace a consultation with a doctor. If you are unwell or worried about a symptom, please see a doctor in person. In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital.
