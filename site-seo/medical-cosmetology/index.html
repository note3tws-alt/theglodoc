Medical cosmetology, explained honestly.

What procedures are, who they suit, and what to ask before you decide.

Medical cosmetology covers skin and hair procedures carried out by trained doctors. Dr. Athmika, a certified consultant medical cosmetologist, explains what a procedure involves and who it suits. Every person's skin and hair are different, so suitability is decided after an assessment, and no result is guaranteed.

Questions this section covers

  • What is medical cosmetology, and how is it different from a salon treatment?
  • How do I know whether a procedure suits my skin or hair?
  • What questions should I ask before any procedure?
  • What are the risks and recovery for common procedures?
  • How do I choose a qualified practitioner?

Dr. Athmika's articles on these questions are written and reviewed by her before they are published here. In the meantime you can follow her on Instagram or enquire about a consultation.
